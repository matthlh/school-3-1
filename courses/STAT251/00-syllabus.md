## The 96 plan for this course, in numbers

**33% of your grade is non-exam work** — clicker (in class, MWF), labs (in person, Fri),
WeBWorK, written assignments and pre-lab quizzes (at home). None of it is a timed closed-book
exam, and everything except the clicker can be checked in office hours or on Piazza before you
submit. There is no reason to lose a single point.

Bank all 33 plus the 1% Piazza bonus = **34 points**. Then:

> 34 + (exams) = 96 → you need **62 out of 67** on the exams = **~92.5%**

Without the bonus you need 94% on exams. **So the Piazza bonus is worth about 1.5 points of exam
performance.** Ten good explanations on a discussion board is a much cheaper way to buy 1.5%
than grinding for an extra two marks on the final. Do it in the first month while the questions
are easy.

the real cost of this course. Sit in the same seat each day (the syllabus asks for it).
